Who is Toxic Dee Modder
on October 11, 2026

Toxic Dee Modder (also known online as TOXIC GAMING) is the official developer handle and digital brand of Andile Pat Mtolo, a South African software developer, application analyst, and founder of Alysium Corporation Studios Inc. Based in South Africa, he specializes in mobile application development, networking utilities, and artificial intelligence solutions. Under his developer alias and studio banner, he has created and published a wide range of digital tools, including Android utilities like GTA LAG FIXER, AndroidIDE Coder, and Pxel Notes; networking platforms such as neuraVPN, ACE Injector, JD Tunnel Socks VPN, and ACE Global VPN; as well as web-based AI platforms like NeuraPrompt AI and NeuraStudy AI.
